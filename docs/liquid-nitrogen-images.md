# Grafiki do pokazu ciekłego azotu

Wygenerowano wbudowanym ImageGen 2 listopada 2026. Grafiki ilustrują propozycję atrakcji, nie dokumentują prawdziwego wydarzenia. Oryginały zachowano w katalogu wygenerowanych obrazów, a poniższe pliki projektu skompresowano do WebP (1536 × 1024).

- `public/images/ciekly-azot/pokaz.webp` — główna grafika pokazu, używana na trzech podstronach, w banerach i formularzu.
- `public/images/ciekly-azot/mrozny-kwiat.webp` — przykładowa mroźna przemiana kwiatu.

## Prompty

### nitrogen-hero

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide landscape hero photograph for the Polish children's entertainment website Nallipop, aspect ratio 3:2. Primary request: a spectacular yet realistic liquid nitrogen science demonstration with a joyful, premium event atmosphere. Scene: spacious well-ventilated science stage, deep midnight navy background, cyan edge lighting and a subtle lavender glow. An enthusiastic adult female science presenter wearing a white laboratory coat, clear face shield and loose cryogenic protective gloves stands behind a professional demonstration table on the right half of the picture. An open stainless steel cryogenic vessel rests securely on the table and thick beautiful white condensation billows gently over its rim and across the foreground, sculptural clouds with fine detail. No pouring or splashing. A small group of fascinated children in the distant left background, clearly well away from the demonstration table behind a barrier, smiling and observing, no touching. Composition: panoramic editorial cinematic scene, table and presenter as focal point in the center-right, dark airy negative space at left for website headline overlay. Photorealistic natural human anatomy, realistic faces, tactile materials, no dramatic explosion, no ice cream, no flames, no sealed containers, no text, no watermarks, no logos.

### nitrogen-flower

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape editorial detail photograph for a children's liquid nitrogen science show website, aspect ratio 3:2. Primary request: a breathtaking delicate pink rose covered in tiny sparkling frost crystals, held by long professional stainless steel tongs just above an open stainless steel cryogenic demonstration vessel. The tongs extend from the right edge, no hands or people in frame. Wisps of beautiful dense white condensation surround the flower and curl around the bottom third, realistic ice microtexture. Dark midnight navy background with luminous turquoise rim lighting and a faint lavender accent, high-end science museum campaign photograph, elegant composition with the rose on the center-left and ample breathing room. Shallow depth of field, crisp frost detail, visually wondrous and scientifically plausible. No text, no watermark, no logos, no shattering fragments, no touching cryogenic objects, no food, no fantasy objects.
